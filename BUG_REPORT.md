# Bug Report

Use this file for the final findings after real calls are complete.

## Template

### Bug: Short descriptive title

- Severity: Low | Medium | High | Critical
- Call: `artifacts/transcripts/<call-id>.txt` at approximate timestamp
- Scenario: `<scenario-id>`
- Evidence: Quote or summarize the relevant exchange
- Why it matters: Explain patient impact, safety risk, or product quality issue
- Expected behavior: What the agent should have done instead

## Candidate Areas To Watch

- Scheduling outside office hours or without confirming availability
- Medication refill promises without clinician review boundaries
- Insurance hallucinations when coverage is uncertain
- Failure to escalate urgent symptoms
- Inconsistent appointment times, locations, or patient identity
- Poor recovery after interruption or unclear requests
